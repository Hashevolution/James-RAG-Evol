## 1. Why this doc exists

v0.5 close (PR #862) shipped with a **two-fork v0.6 entry contract**:

> v0.6 entry requires either:
> 1. **LOI signed** → vertical pack scoping conversation begins.
>    Stream D (legal-vertical or similar) becomes scope-eligible.
> 2. **No LOI within 6 months → reassess** → strategy pivot. v0.5
>    work remains the mother-platform consolidation baseline;
>    v0.6 cycle re-scopes around the next strategic direction.

Neither has resolved. The interval is a productive **"v0.5 closed,
v0.6 not yet entered" state** during which mother-platform
hardening continues — and 23 additional PRs (#863 – #885) have
landed since the close handover did exactly that.

The next session needs a place to land that's neither a stale
"close handover" view (which reads as if work stopped at #862) nor
a premature "v0.6 entry doc" (which can't honestly be written
until a gate resolves). This skeleton is that landing place.

remains the only path:

### 4.1 Fork A — LOI signed

If a customer LOI is signed:

1. The next session writes `docs/handovers/v0.6-entry-<date>.md`
   that scopes the vertical pack for that customer (per the
   handover §8 mechanical entry checklist 4).
2. **Track D** becomes scope-eligible. The vertical-pack work
   queue from §5.4 (first vertical pack build → 6-month customer
   pilot measurement → Dim F evidence collection) starts.
3. **G8.d** becomes scope-eligible. The capability grant
   workflow lands with that customer's pack as the first
   `rule_one_exemption_granted` target.
4. **F.2 CR.e** customer-specific theming becomes scope-eligible
   in the post-LOI marketing layer.
5. CLAUDE.md rule #1 stays in force for **any other vertical**.
   Only the LOI-scoped vertical is exempt.

### 4.2 Fork B — no LOI within 6 months → reassess

If no LOI by 6 months after v0.5 close (2026-12-12):

1. The next session writes a strategy-pivot doc in
   `docs/strategy/v0.6-reassess-<date>.md` that names the new
   strategic direction (handover §4 phrasing: "v0.6 cycle
   re-scopes around the next strategic direction").
2. v0.5 mother-platform work stays the baseline.
3. The reassess decision document NAMES what v0.6 entry looks
   like under the new direction.
4. CLAUDE.md rule #1 stays in force — the no-vertical-content
   rule does not loosen because the strategy shifted; only an
   LOI exemption (Fork A) opens a vertical.

**Neither fork should be assumed by the next session**. Both
must resolve via operator decision.
