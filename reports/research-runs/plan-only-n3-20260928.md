## 2. The latency number needs care

Every pair has NOPLAN faster — −4.7, −0.9, −12.2 s — but the magnitude
swings with the timeouts, which fell in r2 and r3 of **both** arms
(PLAN r3: 9, NOPLAN r2: 8). The median-of-medians gap, −8.9 s, is
inflated by them.

**The clean estimate is the r1 pair, where neither arm timed out:
−4.7 s (≈ 12%).** That agrees in order of magnitude with the ~2.8 s the
09-24 decomposition attributed to plan.

GPU state was steady (mean SM clock 2604–2748 MHz), and the latency
drifted *up* across the session in both arms (PLAN 40.6 → 46.5 →
49.8 s). The interleaving is what keeps that drift out of the
comparison.

## 3. Correction — timeouts are not a first-run artifact

`cognitive-stages-n3-20260927.md` §4 concluded that timeouts
*"concentrate in the first run of each configuration"* and called them
a cold-configuration artifact. Here they appear **mid- and late-session**
(r2, r3) and never in r1. What survives from 09-27 is the part that
mattered: timeouts occur in both arms and are not caused by the stage
under test. What does not survive is the "first run" pattern — it was
the shape of one session, not a rule. Timeouts are intermittent
host / LLM-server events.

## 5. What this licenses — and what it does not

**Licensed**

- On step7, plan changes nothing measurable — not the five axes, not
  verify's catches — and costs ~5 s/query.

**Not licensed**

- ❌ **"Turn plan off" — not on this evidence.** plan's design intent is
  *decomposing multi-step questions*, and step7 is almost entirely
  single-hop. This is the stage whose purpose the suite tests least.
  Unlike reflect, which carried a large cost *and* a traced harm (Q19),
  plan has a small cost and no traced effect in either direction. The
  honest reading is "untested for what it is for", not "useless".
- ❌ The −8.9 s median gap as plan's cost (§2).

**For the operator**: like reflect, `JAMES_ENABLE_PLANNER` is **off by
default in the code** and the deployment `.env` turns it on. Leaving it
on costs ~5 s/query; the case for turning it off is weaker than
reflect's, and the multi-hop suite is the measurement that would settle
it.
